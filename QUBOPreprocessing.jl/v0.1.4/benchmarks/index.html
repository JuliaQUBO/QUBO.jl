    QUBOLib, QOBLIB & MINLPLib Corpus Benchmarks

    This page summarizes the full-corpus evaluation of QUBOPreprocessing.jl across all 6,292 benchmark instances and 2,134 bitstring solution records (2,447 total solution entries) in QUBOLib.jl (v0.2.4-data+6, Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.23757887), QOBLIB (16a166ee67c24c112551c803aab5394743b815b5), QPLIB, and MINLPLib (9472b011).

    QUBOLib, QOBLIB, QPLIB, and MINLPLib corpus overview

    QOBLIB contains 433 unconstrained QUBO .qs.xz models across four classes: Market Split (01-marketsplit, 156 instances), Low-Autocorrelation Binary Sequences (02-labs, 99 instances), Portfolio Optimization (06-portfolio, 128 instances), and Maximum Independent Set (04-steiner / 07-independentset, 50 instances). All 433 / 433QOBLIB QUBO models match the qoblib collection in QUBOLib.jl with 100% exact identity across variable count $n$, linear terms, quadratic terms, objective offset, scale, domain (bool), and sense (min).

    In QUBOLib.jlv0.2.4-data+6, the 23QPLIB binary quadratic (QBB) instances incorporate the upstream $\frac{1}{2} x^\top Q^0 x$ quadratic scaling correction (0.5 * c), bringing all 23 .sol records into exact 0.0 agreement with their declared upstream objectives (15MINLPLibsporttournament22..50 instances, 3MINLPLib 2D/3D Ising spin glasses, and 5BiqMac instances, with 11 certified globally optimal). In addition, QUBOLib.jlv0.2.4-data+6 includes 29 pure binary unconstrained QUBO instances from MINLPLib (2autocorr_bern20-03, 25-03, 20 D-Wave chimera_*, and 7sporttournament06..20, with 18 proven globally optimal and 100%$\mathbb{Z}_2$ gauge invariance after exact 0.1 scale factorization via integerize_scale).

    Coercive local-field & witness-guided probing (probe_variables!)

    Using coercive_field_witness(ws) with order = :coercive autonomously deduces 14 strong and 12 weak variable fixings on frustrated MIS instances (chesapeake, football, mammalia-kangaroo-interactions, insecta-ant-colony1-day38). Supplying each suboptimal heuristic solution as an upper-bound witness (witness = x_subopt) to probe_variables! after strong preprocessing deduces 225 additional certified variable fixings across 58 MIS solutions (60 on es60fst03, 56 on es60fst04, 45 on es60fst01, 44 on es60fst02, and 20 on es60fst08).
