    QUBOLib, QOBLIB & MINLPLib Corpus Benchmarks

    This page summarizes the full-corpus evaluation of QUBOPreprocessing.jl across all 6,292 benchmark instances and 2,134 bitstring solution records (2,447 total solution entries) in QUBOLib.jl (v0.2.4-data+6, Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.23757887), QOBLIB (16a166ee67c24c112551c803aab5394743b815b5), QPLIB, and MINLPLib (9472b011).

    QUBOLib, QOBLIB, QPLIB, and MINLPLib corpus overview

    QOBLIB contains 433 unconstrained QUBO .qs.xz models across four classes: Market Split (01-marketsplit, 156 instances), Low-Autocorrelation Binary Sequences (02-labs, 99 instances), Portfolio Optimization (06-portfolio, 128 instances), and Maximum Independent Set (04-steiner / 07-independentset, 50 instances). All 433 / 433QOBLIB QUBO models match the qoblib collection in QUBOLib.jl with 100% exact identity across variable count $n$, linear terms, quadratic terms, objective offset, scale, domain (bool), and sense (min).

    In QUBOLib.jlv0.2.4-data+6, the 23QPLIB binary quadratic (QBB) instances incorporate the upstream $\frac{1}{2} x^\top Q^0 x$ quadratic scaling correction (0.5 * c), bringing all 23 .sol records into exact 0.0 agreement with their declared upstream objectives (15MINLPLibsporttournament22..50 instances, 3MINLPLib 2D/3D Ising spin glasses, and 5BiqMac instances, with 11 certified globally optimal). In addition, QUBOLib.jlv0.2.4-data+6 includes 29 pure binary unconstrained QUBO instances from MINLPLib (2autocorr_bern20-03, 25-03, 20 D-Wave chimera_*, and 7sporttournament06..20, with 18 proven globally optimal and 100%$\mathbb{Z}_2$ gauge invariance after opt-in decimal 0.1 scale factorization via integerize_scale(model; atol=1e-12)).

    Coercive local fields, spin-field alignment ($m \approx 0.60$), and coercive_field_witness across MQLib, QPLIB, MINLPLib, and QOBLIB

    Transforming a binary QUBO $F(x) = \alpha\left(\beta + \sum_i L_i x_i + \sum_{i < j} \tilde{Q}_{ij} x_i x_j\right)$ into an equivalent minimization Ising spin Hamiltonian $H(s) = E_0 + \sum_i h_i s_i + \sum_{i < j} J_{ij} s_i s_j$ under $s_i = 2 x_i - 1 \in \{-1, +1\}$ (with sense sign $\sigma = +1$ for :min and $\sigma = -1$ for :max) induces local coercive fields $h_i = \frac{\sigma}{2} L_i + \frac{\sigma}{4} \sum_{j \ne i} \tilde{Q}_{ij}, \qquad J_{ij} = \frac{\sigma}{4}\tilde{Q}_{ij}.$ For zero-mean, finite-variance random QUBO matrices with symmetric off-diagonal signs ($\sim 50\%$$\tilde{Q}_{ij} > 0$), spin-glass analysis of the QUBO-to-Ising mapping (S. Boettcher, Phys. Rev. Research1, 033142, 2019, doi:10.1103/PhysRevResearch.1.033142, arXiv:1906.10036, Fig. 2 and §IV.B.1) reports numerical ground-state field magnetization near $m = \frac{1}{n}\sum_i s_i^* \operatorname{sgn}(-h_i) \approx 0.60$, corresponding to $\approx 80\%$ ground-state spin-field alignment ($s_i^* h_i < 0$).

    Benchmark methodology and definitions (scripts/coercive_alignment_pilot.jl)

    The script scripts/coercive_alignment_pilot.jl constructs a production Workspace(model; rules = :identity, integerize_scale = false) and evaluates coercive_field_witness across all 611 instances in the classic OR random QUBO families of MQLib (Beasley, GKA, Palubeckis), QPLIB, MINLPLib, and QOBLIB from QUBOLib.jl (v0.2.4-data+8, git-tree-sha1 = db23630c10bf992c60033093eb46067fad78ccaa):

    • Reference solutions (s*), sample coverage, and proven-optimal counts: Reference states $x^*$ are loaded directly from BestSolutions.bitstring in QUBOLib.jl (v0.2.4-data+8). Across all 611 instances, 570 / 611 have dimension-matching reference bitstrings (100% of MQLib, QPLIB, MINLPLib, LABS, Maximum Independent Set, and Portfolio Optimization, plus 115 / 156 of Market Split; the 41Market Split instances without stored bitstrings are included in Inst, h!=0, % Q_ij > 0, and CFW Alignment, and skipped for reference-dependent metrics). The column Inst (Ref / Prov / h!=0) reports total instances, instances with reference bitstrings (Ref), instances flagged proven_optimal = 1 (Prov), and instances with at least one active local field $|h_i| > \max(10^{-12}, 10^{-12}\|J_{i,\cdot}\|_1)$ (h!=0).
    • Active-field alignment: Since $h_i$ defined above already incorporates the optimization sense sign $\sigma$, a spin $s_i \in \{-1, +1\}$ aligns with its coercive field when $s_i h_i < 0$. For instances with $n_{\text{act}} > 0$, spin-field alignment is $\frac{1}{n_{\text{act}}}\sum_{|h_i| > \text{tol}_i} \mathbf{1}[s_i h_i < 0]$, reported both as the per-instance mean ± standard deviation (Ref. Alignment) and pooled across all active spins in the cohort (Spin-Wtd Alignment).
    • Witness parameters and relative optimality gap: Both parallel field alignment + 1-flip steepest descent (Parallel 1-Flip) and coercive_field_witness (CFW, which takes the lower-energy polished state between parallel field alignment and sequential :coercive-ordered conditioned field alignment with $\ell_1$-coupling tie-breaking and $\mathbb{Z}_2$ zero-field anchor symmetry breaking $s_{u_1} = +1$) are run with max_flips = max(500, n) and tie_break = :low. For each instance with a reference bitstring, the relative optimality gap is $100 \times \frac{\max(0, \sigma(F(x^{\text{wit}}) - F(x^*)))}{\max(|F(x^*)|, 10^{-9})}$ (using $\max(1, |\alpha\beta|)$ when $|F(x^*)| \le 10^{-9}$).
    • Spin overlap: Direct (Z2) Overlap reports the mean bitstring agreement $\text{ov} = \frac{100}{n}\sum_{i=1}^n \mathbf{1}[s_i^{\text{CFW}} = s_i^*]$, together with the $\mathbb{Z}_2$-invariant overlap $\max(\text{ov}, 100\% - \text{ov})$ in parentheses where global spin inversion $s \leftrightarrow -s$ is a symmetry (or near-symmetry).

    Four-regime structural interpretation

    1. Regime I — Unconstrained OR Random QUBOs (mqlib Beasley, GKA, Palubeckis & qplib BiqMac OR 5875..5922):
      • Across all four families (131 instances with $h_i \ne 0$ and $49.7\%\text{–}60.8\%$ positive off-diagonal entries), reference ground-state spin-field alignment is 76.17%–83.58% ($m \in [0.523, 0.672]$) and coercive_field_witness spin-field alignment is 78.85%–84.67%, closely matching the $\approx 80\%$ ($m \approx 0.60$) numerical ground-state spin-field alignment reported by Boettcher (Phys. Rev. Research1, 033142, 2019, arXiv:1906.10036, Fig. 2 and §IV.B.1).
      • Sequential conditioned field alignment + 1-flip polish in coercive_field_witness lowers the median relative optimality gap to 0.55%–0.96% across all four families (91.88%, 93.78%, 89.93%, and 89.62% spin overlap with the reference solutions).
    2. Regime II — $\mathbb{Z}_2$ Gauge-Invariant Zero-Field Max-Cut / Ising Encodings (qplibh = 0 & minlplibh = 0):
      • After applying the exact $\frac{1}{2} x^\top Q^0 x$.qplib scaling, 18 / 23qplib instances (15sporttournament22..50 + 3 Ising spin glasses) and all 29 / 29minlplib binary QUBO instances satisfy $L_i + \frac{1}{2}\sum_{j \ne i}\tilde{Q}_{ij} = 0$, i.e. $h_i = 0$ identically (is_gauge_invariant(ws) == true).
      • Separating qplib into the 5 non-zero-field BiqMac OR instances (0.96% median gap, 89.62% overlap) vs. the 18 zero-field Max-Cut/Ising instances (17.17% median gap) resolves why pooled qplib previously appeared to have an 18.41% gap.
      • Within minlplib (29 zero-field instances), coercive_field_witness with $\ell_1$-coupling secondary ordering and zero-field anchor symmetry breaking reduces the overall median gap from 16.25% (parallel 1-flip) to 12.31% (65.53%$\mathbb{Z}_2$-invariant overlap; 49.80% direct overlap):
        • Bernasconi Autocorrelation (2 instances): 0.00% gap (100.00% overlap).
        • D-Wave Chimera MIS (2 instances): 9.62% -> 4.93% median gap (67.89%$\mathbb{Z}_2$-invariant overlap).
        • Sports Tournament 06..20 (7 instances): 16.67% -> 13.64% median gap (62.86%$\mathbb{Z}_2$-invariant overlap).
        • D-Wave Chimera Spin Glass (16 Selby/MGW/RFR instances): 15.68% -> 13.02% median gap (63.66%$\mathbb{Z}_2$-invariant overlap).
    3. Regime III — Asymmetric Penalty-Dominated Combinatorial QUBOs (qoblib MIS & Portfolio Optimization):
      • When quadratic penalty terms have predominantly positive signs (100.0%$Q_{ij} > 0$ in Maximum Independent Set; 67.6%$Q_{ij} > 0$ in Portfolio Optimization), the induced local fields $h_i = \frac{1}{2}L_i + \frac{1}{4}\sum_{j \ne i}\tilde{Q}_{ij} > 0$ push most variables to $x_i = 0$ ($s_i = -1$), driving ground-state spin-field alignment above$81\%$ to 84.00% (93.25% spin-weighted) on MIS and 93.23% (93.65% spin-weighted) on Portfolio Optimization.
      • On Maximum Independent Set, sequential conditioned field alignment in coercive_field_witnesscuts the median optimality gap nearly in half compared to parallel field alignment (30.38% -> 17.67%, 88.10% direct / 88.60%$\mathbb{Z}_2$ spin overlap), because assigning $x_u = 1$ sequentially updates adjacent vertices' conditioned fields $\tilde{h}_v \leftarrow \tilde{h}_v + J_{uv}$ and prevents simultaneously selecting adjacent vertices.
    4. Regime IV — Balanced Demand Cancellation (qoblib Market Split):
      • In Market Split ($\|Ax - b\|^2$ with $b = \lfloor \frac{1}{2} A\mathbf{1}\rfloor$), the linear and quadratic penalty contributions to $h_i = \frac{1}{2} A_i^\top (A\mathbf{1} - 2b)$ cancel to near zero ($\rho_1 \approx 10^{-4}$), yielding 50.04% ± 1.94% ground-state spin-field alignment ($m \approx 0.001$) across the 115 instances with reference solutions, while 1-flip local search reaches within 0.02% of the reference objective.
