    Sound bounds and proof verification

    A solver result and a reduction proof answer different questions. Use this page when another consumer needs to check why a variable was fixed, or when you need to decide whether a captured proof still applies. The user guide shows where verification fits in a complete solve.

    prove_dominance(ws, label) checks one strict unary reduction without changing the workspace. Bounded dominance separately provides the queue, propagation and inference undo trail. The standalone helper provides an interval/exact witness seam and an independently structured verifier, with no mandatory external backend.

    using QUBOPreprocessing
import QUBOTools as QT
ws = Workspace(QT.Model(Dict(:x=>3.0,:y=>0.0),Dict((:x,:y)=>-1.0)))
attempt = prove_dominance(ws,:x; max_exact_work=0)
@assert attempt.method == :interval
@assert verify_certificate(ws,attempt.certificate).valid
@assert isempty(assumptions(ws))
only(attempt.certificate.steps).value
    0

    The exact represented problem

    For stored finite Float64 coefficients, the mathematical objective is $E(x)=\alpha(\beta+\sum_i L_i x_i+\sum_{i<j}Q_{ij}x_i x_j)$ with each Float64 interpreted as its exact binary rational value. Both domains and senses are supported directly; this check does not cast between domains.

    Let $\sigma=\operatorname{sign}(\alpha)$ for minimization and its negative for maximization. Multiplication by its sign normalizes the direction without forming an overflowing scaled coefficient. Zero/negative-zero scale yields no strict proof because all states tie. Under the current fixed values, the normalized derivative is $D_i=\sigma(L_i+\sum_j Q_{ij}x_j)$. For a binary free neighbor, its contribution lies between $\min(0,\sigma Q_{ij})$ and $\max(0,\sigma Q_{ij})$. For a spin neighbor it lies between $-|Q_{ij}|$ and $|Q_{ij}|$. Fixed neighbors contribute their exact value.

    A strictly positive lower bound proves binary 0 / spin -1 in every scoped optimum; a strictly negative upper bound proves 1. For spin, the energy difference from -1 to +1 is twice this derivative, preserving its sign. Ties never produce a strict fixing. Unscaled constants do not affect this test but are bound in the witness and recomputed by the verifier.

    The fast path starts with an enclosure checked against the exact cached field, then expands each Float64 sum outward with prevfloat/nextfloat. Original adjacency coefficients retain their represented Float64 values. It changes no process-global rounding mode. Nonfinite enclosures or an unresolved sign trigger budgeted exact rational fallback; no epsilon or ordinary rounded sum is used as a proof. Exact fields are imported once and updated locally by the workspace; no whole-model BigInt conversion occurs at each numerical check.

    DominanceAttempt reports method, visited adjacency entries, fallback count and exact work. max_work bounds adjacency visits across both passes; max_exact_work reserves the field plus incident coefficient contributions for fallback. A deadline, exhausted budget, zero scale or non-strict bound returns no certificate. Work counts are structural, with integer bit complexity and allocation costs additional. Public proof export copies source/scope explicitly after a successful numeric check; deadline checks surround that atomic copy.

    Guarantee, scope, composition and verifier

    Each reduction step or coordinate map carries an explicit guarantee class queried with step_guarantee and certificate_guarantee, and a persistency tag (:strong or :weak) queried with persistency:

    • :energy_identity (DomainGaugeMap): bijective coordinate and gauge transformation $F(x) = F'(T(x))$ preserving every state and its exact energy.
    • :exact_elimination (EliminationStep): local variable elimination of a bounded-degree (0 <= d <= 3, default max_degree=2 in prove_elimination, eliminate_variable! and eliminate_low_degree!) variable $u$ certifying $F'(y) = \operatorname{opt}_{x_u} F(y, x_u)$ (equivalent in normalized Ising spin coordinates to $-|h_u + \sum_{j \in \partial u} J_{uj} s_j|$) across all $2^d$ boundary assignments of neighbors, with an exact argmin reconstruction table (tie_break=:low or :high) and exact quadratic polynomial deltas (constant_delta, linear_deltas, quadratic_deltas). For d == 3 (Boettcher–Davidheiser (2008) Section II Rule V Eq. (4) zero-field Ising star–triangle reduction), $u$ must have three distinct free neighbors with nonzero couplings and an exact zero effective Ising field $h_u = 0$ at the moment of application so that spin-flip symmetry makes the local minimum an even function of its three neighbors (pure constant + pair representation in Ising coordinates, with zero cubic interaction $\alpha_{123} = 0$ and zero neighbor Ising field shifts). Nonzero cubic interactions are refused with :nonquadratic_elimination, and nonzero center effective fields at d == 3 are refused with :nonzero_field. unique_argmin records whether the minimizing value of $u$ is strict on all boundary states. Higher-degree variables are skipped/refused (:degree_too_high) with no higher-order truncation.
    • :optimum_preserving_restriction (DominanceStep, RelationStep, ProbingStep, RoofDualityStep, SubmodularCutStep, MaxCutStep): unary value fixing (DominanceStep), signed pair contraction $s_u = \sigma_{\text{orig}} s_v$ (RelationStep, proved via single-endpoint :interval / :exact coupling dominance or Glover–Lewis–Kochenberger (2018) Theorems 3.1–3.4 cross-endpoint :glk_pair implication bounds with favoring coupling $-\sigma \tilde{J}_{uv} > 0$), trial-branch probing fixing (ProbingStep, proved via prove_probing / apply_probing! / probe_variables! when the trial branch lower bound $L$ after reversible trial conditioning and bounded trial dominance propagation strictly exceeds the verified parent-scope feasible witness upper bound $U$, $L > U$), exact implication-network roof-duality strong persistency fixing (RoofDualityStep, proved via prove_roof_duality / apply_roof_duality! from an independently verified exact rational maximum $S$–$T$ flow $f$ and dual minimum cuts $(\mathcal{R}_S, V \setminus \mathcal{R}_S)$ and $(V \setminus \mathcal{R}_T, \mathcal{R}_T)$ where literal $2u - 1 \in \mathcal{R}_S \land 2u \in \mathcal{R}_T$ for $s_u = +1$ or $2u \in \mathcal{R}_S \land 2u - 1 \in \mathcal{R}_T$ for $s_u = -1$), direct gauge-submodular component cut fixing (SubmodularCutStep, proved via prove_submodular_cut / apply_submodular_cut! on a closed active residual component $C$ with verified bipartite spin gauge $\sigma_a \in \{-1, +1\}$ making all active couplings $\tilde{J}'_{ab} \le 0$ and verified $(|C| + 2)$-vertex maximum $S$–$T$ flow and minimum cut), or weighted Max-Cut edge/triangle restriction (MaxCutStep, proved via prove_maxcut / apply_maxcut! on $G_{\text{MC}} = (\{0\} \cup F, E_{\\text{cut}}, w)$ from the exact subset-flip margin $M_{ab}(\sigma) = -\sigma J_{ab} + \sum_{c \in C_{\text{tri}}} \gamma_{abc}(\sigma) - \sum_{d \notin \{a, b\} \cup C_{\text{tri}}} |J_{ad}|$ with verified external triangle slack $R_c^{(ab)}$). Tagged :strong when every optimum of the current residual satisfies the restriction, or :weak when at least one optimum of the current residual satisfies the restriction.

    Each DominanceCertificate (aliased as ReductionCertificate) owns a source snapshot, generation, branch-specific checkpoint, explicit caller assumptions and an ordered chain of DominanceStep, RelationStep and EliminationStep records. Because steps are composed sequentially:

    1. If $F_k$ preserves all (or at least one) optima of $F_{k-1}$ and step $k$ is certified on the evolved residual $F_k$, then $F_{k+1}$ preserves all (or at least one) optima of $F_0$.
    2. Weak restrictions are not closed under simultaneous application: if two weak steps hold individually on $F_k$, the second must be re-certified on $F_{k+1}$ after the first updates the residual. Default propagate!(ws) remains strong-only (allow_weak=false); apply_weak_batch! and propagate!(ws; allow_weak=true) apply weak steps sequentially on the evolving residual.
    3. Reconstruction (reconstruct) walks reduction_chain in reverse application order so every DominanceStep, RelationStep and EliminationStep reads only survivor/representative/boundary values that have already been reconstructed.

    verify_certificate(ws, certificate) compares actual source data as well as generation, checks the live caller scope and exact current trail-tip checkpoint (generation, epoch, depth and serial), then independently evolves the exact original polynomial from raw source storage step by step across caller assumptions, DominanceStep, RelationStep and EliminationStep records. Later caller assumptions restrict still-free variables; they are not retroactively substituted into earlier bound/constant evidence. It does not trust cached fields, adjacency, constant or queue and does not call the producer's bound/conditioning helper. Exact bounds must match exact recomputation; interval bounds must enclose the exact range and prove the claimed strict sign; elimination steps must match all $2^d \le 8$ boundary states and argmin table values. A wider but still sound enclosure remains mathematically valid; forged strict bounds, values, signs, tables, deltas, constants, predecessors, reordered steps, unconditioned weak batches and source identity are rejected. This is mathematical verification, not just hash authentication.

    root = checkpoint(ws)
assume!(ws,:y,0)
conditional = prove_dominance(ws,:x).certificate
@assert verify_certificate(ws,conditional).valid
rollback!(ws,root)
@assert !verify_certificate(ws,conditional).valid

    A branch assumption does not certify that the branch contains an original global optimum. Removing/replacing its scope invalidates applicability, including when the same values are later reapplied on another branch. Source/penalty edits require reset! and a new generation. A root-global proof never depends on caller assumptions. Heuristic solvers and source ToQUBO feasibility retain their own separate correctness requirements.

    Verification is an explicit boundary: source identity costs O(n+m), and each step scans original storage, potentially O(steps(n+m)) with sparse input and O(stepsn²) for dense storage. term_visits reports charged storage work, including a reserved full scan if interruption follows storage reconstruction. Work/deadline exhaustion always yields valid=false. Proof capture, verification and residual materialization costs must be included in consumer measurements; the cheap witness helper alone is not whole-solve cost evidence.

    A valid fixing proof does not authorize rounding the residual. Workspace Float64 export still rejects coefficients/constants that cannot be represented exactly; exact Rational{BigInt} transport is an explicit supported alternative. Re-evaluating a lifted state's Float64 energy cannot repair an unsound transport. Likewise, as verified by the Issue #21 exact compression audit (audit_compression, qubolite_compression_candidates, compression_counterexamples), in-place coefficient clipping or dynamic-range compression is excluded from ReductionCertificate because closed-endpoint updates introduce suboptimal ties, negative-coupling updates can lose all original optima, non-optimal energies cease to shift by a constant, and every sound 1-hop strict-interior case is already subsumed with strictly better sparsification and an exact constant-shift certificate by DominanceStep (#14) or RelationStep (#15). Domain- and gauge-transported proofs (prove_dominance(ws, label; map=...), export_certificate(ws; map=...) and transport_certificate(ws, cert, map)) bind the same source generation and caller assumptions; verify_certificate independently recomputes the converted and gauged polynomial from raw source storage and rejects wrong gauges, mismatched domains, stale maps or altered variable ordering. Run examples/certificates.jl, examples/domain.jl and scripts/certificate_costs.jl for executable examples and separate numeric-path, proof-export and verification measurements.
